# Title
xMBA Startup-Coach & Agile Business Expert (SAQ, ISO/IEC 17024)

# Category
certification

# Problem
Senior technical leaders often need structured knowledge in agile business practices, innovation methods, and startup execution beyond pure engineering. A formal program can validate breadth across product, customer discovery, legal context, and go-to-market.

# My role
I completed the SAQ Swiss Association for Quality certification program “xMBA Agile Business & Startup Skills” and earned the title “xMBA Startup-Coach and Agile Business Expert”. The certification is a personnel certification aligned with ISO/IEC 17024:2012.

# What I built
The program covers megatrends, key technologies (AI, data analytics, Web 3.0), client-centric design and interviews, legal frameworks for agile companies, design thinking and prototyping, applied AI tools/apps, startup financing, cyber risk defense strategies, cost/revenue models, and marketing/branding/communication. The qualification procedure is a written multiple-choice exam with 200 questions in 90 minutes, requiring at least 80% of points.

# Scale and impact
The certificate is valid from 02.06.2025 to 01.06.2028 and can be renewed with evidence of continued competence. It complements my technical background by formalizing business, product, and startup execution frameworks that I already apply in cross-functional delivery. Certificate number 25ABSS1591478/1.

# Tech stack
Training was delivered in German and English, over 16 days across 4 weeks, and used practical simulations under time pressure with modern digital tools. The certification body is SAQ Swiss Association for Quality (Berne).

# Key decisions and trade-offs
Formal certification trades time and exam constraints for an externally recognized signal and a shared vocabulary with business stakeholders. I use it to strengthen product thinking, customer discovery, and risk-aware execution in technical programs rather than as a substitute for hands-on experience.
