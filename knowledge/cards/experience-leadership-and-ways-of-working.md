# Category
experience

# Problem
AI and platform programs fail when leadership is disconnected from technical reality, or when engineering decisions are made without validation and operational ownership. Distributed teams also need clear alignment on priorities and interfaces.

# My role
I have led international R&D teams and Agile processes, including roadmap ownership and delivery discipline in a distributed context. My style combines hands-on engineering with structured decision-making, mentoring, and clear accountability for outcomes.

# What I built
I establish system boundaries, define interface contracts, and set up delivery practices (CI/CD, testing, observability expectations) so teams can move fast without breaking production. I also support onboarding by making system knowledge explicit, which is a recurring theme across my projects (e.g., Decreen).

# Scale and impact
I have experience acting as Scrum Master in a core database engine team and leading R&D in a managed security services organization. These roles required balancing exploration (research/ML) with production delivery and operational reliability.

# Tech stack
Agile methods: Scrum and Kanban. Delivery tooling: CI/CD pipelines (e.g., Jenkins), code hosting and planning tools (GitHub/Azure DevOps/Rally), and documentation/collaboration platforms (Confluence).

# Key decisions and trade-offs
I prefer making decisions under uncertainty by separating reversible from irreversible choices, validating key assumptions early, and instrumenting systems to learn from real usage. I also treat responsible AI practices as engineering requirements, not policy documents, by embedding guardrails and evaluation into delivery.
