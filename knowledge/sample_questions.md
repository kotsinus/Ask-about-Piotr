# Sample Questions for "Ask About Piotr" AI Demo

This document contains example questions users may ask the AI assistant.
Some questions are expected to be answered based on existing knowledge cards.
Others are intentionally outside the curated knowledge base and should trigger
a controlled refusal with low confidence.
